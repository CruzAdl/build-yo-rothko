### The Drills (How to Strike the Felt)

1. **The Primer**: Choose your format. Vertical, horizontal, or altar. Touch the weave. Then lock it. Once the first slab kisses the ground, locked forever the primer is. Yes.
2. **The Dual Six-Dot Pods**: Float, they do. Drag them smoothly by their skin across the court. Tap the six dots to collapse into tiny pulsing discs when the chatter of the ego grows loud in the locker room. Tap them again to expand. Hitting `Escape` works too, hmm.
3. **The Slabs**: Drag them wide. Stretch the handles. Let the edges bleed. Soft light, dry scumble, or thick impasto—much tension there is between crimson and plum. 
4. **The Bake**: Hit `Enter` or press `Place Shape`. Baked. Irreversible. Like a dropped serve on match point.
5. **The Solvent (`Restart Painting` / `- Remove Last Shape`)**: Strip an unplaced study away, you may. Scrape the entire surface down with solvent, you can. But remember, the void does. Kept, the tally of your ruined studies is. 
6. **The Light Room**: Click `Work is Done`. Stand back three paces in the dim chapel air. Aim the spotlight across the plaster wall. 
7. **The Micro-Plaque**: Small beside the hung painting it sits, realistic and modest. Click it once, and expand to readable size it will. Edit your `Name`, your title, and your tally of destroyed studies. Immutable, `2026` and `Oil on Canvas` remain. Click it again, and shrink back down it does.
8. **The Snapshot**: Press `Take Picture`. A shutter flash, and down comes the high-resolution archival installation shot into your storage.
